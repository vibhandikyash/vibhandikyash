## About Me

Most of my work is end-to-end product engineering for B2B platforms. That usually means taking a fragmented manual process, designing the data model and execution architecture around it, integrating the AI where it earns its place, and shipping it as a product real teams operate daily.

- **AI systems in production:** multi-provider LLM orchestration, agent frameworks, MCP tooling, RAG, computer vision, and voice
- **Platform architecture:** event-driven microservices, durable workflow execution, queueing, multi-tenancy, and RBAC
- **Full-stack delivery:** Next.js/React product surfaces backed by NestJS, FastAPI, and Node services
- **Domains:** sales and growth automation, HR tech, logistics, healthcare operations, ESG reporting, physical security, and e-commerce

## Selected Work

Client engagements, described without naming names.

### Growth Automation SaaS
Growth teams were running lead research, outreach, content, CRM, and analytics across fragmented tooling. Built a platform unifying them: a multi-model AI agent framework with 130+ specialized agents, a drag-and-drop workflow builder with 50+ action nodes and approval gates, custom MCP servers for extensible AI tooling, and 30+ third-party integrations. Event-driven microservices architecture sustaining 1,000+ concurrent workflow executions.

`Next.js` `React` `NestJS` `Kafka` `MongoDB` `ClickHouse` `LangGraph` `Kubernetes`

### Computer Vision Platform
Turning a passive camera estate into an active safety and security system. Built the model-development foundation and production inference architecture: YOLO detectors fine-tuned per site for people, PPE, vehicles, plates, fire and smoke; temporal transformer models for behaviour a single frame cannot resolve; ANPR with perspective rectification and regional syntax validation. Multi-model orchestration runs up to 20 models against one video job, deployable to cloud, on-premise, or edge.

`Python` `PyTorch` `YOLO` `VideoMAE` `ONNX` `TensorRT` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL`

### AI Outbound Voice Agent
Sales reps were spending 70% of their time dialing and leaving voicemails. Built an autonomous outbound calling platform handling introductions, qualification, objection handling, and calendar booking, with a post-call layer for collateral delivery, calendar invites, and sentiment analysis. Campaign setup dropped from 2+ hours to under 10 minutes; live campaigns run at a 25% pickup rate with meetings booked and collateral delivered without a manual call.

`React` `FastAPI` `MongoDB` `LangChain` `ElevenLabs` `Twilio`

### E-Commerce Automation SaaS
Merchants were running stores through disconnected tools, manual processes, and specialist contractors. Built a full-stack AI automation platform with nine modules covering support, product page generation, ad creation, social scheduling, and profit analysis. Over 90% of routine tickets are handled without human intervention, and a product URL becomes a review-ready ad campaign in under 15 minutes.

`Next.js` `React` `TypeScript` `Supabase` `Google Gemini` `n8n` `Commerce & Ads APIs`

### ESG Reporting Platform
Sustainability data lived across spreadsheets, invoices, receipts, and databases, reconciled by hand into reporting templates. Built a platform that ingests unstructured and semi-structured data without forcing fixed templates, using GPT-4 schema mapping with review safeguards, connectors for PostgreSQL/MySQL/SQL Server/AWS RDS, and a KPI engine generating 20+ workforce and emissions metrics. 500-row Excel files process end to end in under a minute.

`Next.js` `Python` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `Azure OpenAI` `Azure Key Vault`

### Multilingual Call Analytics
Clinic managers were reviewing patient call quality by manual sampling and subjective judgement. Built a call intelligence system: webhook ingestion, asynchronous recording processing, and PII masking that strips names, phone numbers, and identity numbers before anything reaches an LLM. Analysis scores agent behaviour and surfaces missed conversions and unresolved complaints across Gujarati, Hindi, and English.

`Next.js` `Python` `FastAPI` `Celery` `PostgreSQL` `Sarvam STT` `IndicNER`

### Recruitment SaaS
Hiring teams were working across disconnected tools for sourcing, screening, interviews, assessments, and scheduling. Built a multi-tenant platform bringing them into one workflow, with an AI recruitment assistant, automated pre-screening and video interviews, live coding assessments, and durable workflow orchestration.

`NestJS` `NX Monorepo` `React` `PostgreSQL` `PgVector` `Temporal` `LangChain` `AWS`
